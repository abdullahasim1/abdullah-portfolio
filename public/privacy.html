Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Abdullah Bin Asim ("I", "me", "my") collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit abdullah-asim-dev.vercel.app (the "Site").

1. Information I Collect

Analytics (opt-in): If you consent, Plausible Analytics collects anonymized data about your visit — pages viewed, referrer, country, device type, and session duration. No personal identifiers, no cookies, no cross-site tracking. You can decline via the cookie banner.

Contact Form: When you submit the contact form, I receive your name, email, subject, and message via Web3Forms (a third-party form handler). I use this only to respond to your inquiry.

Command Palette / Clipboard: When you use the "Copy Email" action, your browser's Clipboard API is used locally — no data leaves your device.

2. How I Use Your Information

  • To reply to contact form submissions
  • To understand site usage (aggregated, anonymized analytics)
  • To improve the Site and fix issues

I do not sell your data. I do not use it for advertising or retargeting.

3. Third-Party Services

Plausible Analytics

Privacy-friendly analytics (GDPR/CCPA compliant). No cookies, no personal data. Data Policy

Web3Forms

Form submission handler. Your message is emailed to me directly. Privacy Policy

GitHub API

Public repository data displayed in the Projects section. Privacy Statement

Vercel (Hosting)

Static hosting and edge network. Privacy Policy

4. Analytics & Consent

The Site uses Plausible Analytics, which is cookie-less and does not store personal data. On first visit, you'll see a consent banner — you may accept or decline. Your choice is stored in localStorage (key: aa-analytics-consent). You can change it anytime by clearing site data.

5. Data Retention

  • Contact form messages: retained in my email inbox until I delete them (typically 1–2 years)
  • Plausible analytics: aggregated data retained per their policy (max 12 months)
  • LocalStorage consent flag: persists until you clear it

6. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

You have the right to:

  • Access personal data I hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion
  • Object to processing
  • Withdraw consent for analytics at any time

To exercise these rights, email me at abdullah.gc.18@gmail.com.

7. Security

The Site is served over HTTPS with HSTS. Content Security Policy (CSP) restricts resource loading. No passwords or sensitive data are stored on the Site.

8. Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed at children under 16. I do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

I may update this policy occasionally. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect changes. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
